Clankpit Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026

Oort Labs develops Clankpit. You can play without an account. Ad-free Android versions, including the closed test, have no ads or Google Play checkout. In those versions, Clankpit does not transmit player data to Oort Labs or to an advertising or analytics service.

Game data on your device

Clankpit saves your settings, progress, unlocks and playtest statistics in the game's private app storage. For example, it records how many sessions you play, how long it takes to reach your first match and which newly unlocked fighters you use. These files allow you to resume play and keep a local record of playtest activity. Android restricts other apps' access to this storage. You can erase these files in Android Settings → Apps → Clankpit → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the game. Clearing storage also removes your progress.

Optional rewarded ads

In versions with rewarded ads enabled, Clankpit uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check which privacy choices apply when you open the game. When ads are requested, Google AdMob collects and shares data that can include your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The Mobile Ads SDK encrypts these transfers in transit. Where available, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review your choices.

Optional Workshop purchases

In versions with purchases enabled, Google Play processes payment. Clankpit checks signed purchase records on your device and sends purchase acknowledgements to Google Play so you can unlock and restore cosmetics. Oort Labs does not receive your card details. Google handles service data, including retention and deletion of Google Play and advertising records, under its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact and deletion

Email Oort Labs at oortlabsgames@gmail.com with a privacy question or to request deletion of a support message. If you contact us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send through Gmail. We use that information to respond and keep it only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. Game files are stored on your device, so you can delete them directly using the steps above. If we update this policy, we post the updated revision date in the app and on our online privacy policy page.
